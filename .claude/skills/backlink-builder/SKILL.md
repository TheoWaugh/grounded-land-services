# Backlink + citation builder (research and drafts only)

Read `../seo-optimizer/business-facts.md` first. Use the exact NAP from it.

Hard rules: no buying links, no PBNs, no link exchanges at scale, no
spam comments, no automated submissions. Claude researches and drafts;
the owner submits and sends.

1. Citation audit (local SEO foundation). For each, use web search to check
   whether a listing exists and whether NAP matches:
   Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB,
   Nextdoor, Facebook, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, Houzz, Yellow Pages,
   Manta, local chambers of commerce in each service city.
2. Local link prospects (use web search; find real, current URLs):
   - Chambers of commerce and business associations in each city
   - Texas / local trade associations (land improvement, builders, ag)
   - Home builders, custom-home builders, realtors who sell raw land,
     surveyors, excavation/septic/fencing/pool companies (partner pages)
   - Equipment dealers/brands whose gear you run (customer spotlights)
   - Local news, community blogs, HOA/ranch newsletters (wildfire
     mitigation, cedar clearing, oak wilt stories = newsworthy angles)
   - Sponsorship opportunities (youth sports, FFA/4-H, county fairs)
   - Resource pages that list local contractors or wildfire-prep resources
   - Podcast/guest-post opportunities on land, homesteading, ranch topics
3. Link-worthy assets on our site to pitch (propose, and build if simple):
   cost guide with a table, permit guide by county, wildfire defensible-space
   checklist, before/after gallery.
4. Write `seo-reports/backlinks-<date>.csv` with columns:
   type, name, url, contact_page, why_relevant, angle, priority, status
   (status = "not started"). Only include URLs you actually found.
5. Write `seo-reports/outreach-drafts-<date>.md`: a short, personalized
   email/DM for each top-15 prospect (no templates that read like spam),
   plus a 1-page "citation checklist" with the exact NAP to paste.
6. Also draft a simple review-request text/email the owner can send
   happy customers (reviews are the #1 map-pack lever). No incentives.
