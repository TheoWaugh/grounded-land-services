**Land Clearing**
Excavator land clearing for pasture, homesites, and ranches. We grub cedar, mesquite, and brush, roots and all. $1,500–$2,500 per acre depending on density, terrain, rock, and access. About 4–7 acres per day. Free site visits.

**Forestry Mulching**
One-pass forestry mulching that grinds cedar, brush, and small trees into mulch where they stand, with no burn piles and no hauling. Selective clearing around oaks and pecans. $2,500–$3,000 per day, about 1–3 acres per day depending on brush.

**Rock Removal**
Rock removal for pasture, yards, and building sites. We rake and haul surface rock and leave the ground ready for seed or construction. $2,000–$4,000 per acre depending on rock size and volume. Free estimates.

**Cedar Tree Removal**
Cedar (Ashe juniper) removal across the Hill Country. We mulch or grub cedar to open up pasture, give oaks back their water, and cut fire risk. Selective clearing available.

**Underbrushing**
Clears the brush, small cedar, and dead limbs under your trees while keeping the oaks and pecans you want. Opens up views, access, and walking room on wooded lots.

**Rock Crushing**
On-site rock crushing that turns surface rock into usable ground or base material instead of hauling it off. $3,000–$4,000+ per day. Available in the Hill Country and Austin area.

**Fire Breaks**
Fire breaks and defensible space cut with forestry mulching equipment to slow wildfire around homes, barns, and property lines.

**Tree Pile Shredding**
We grind old brush and burn piles into mulch on site. Great during burn bans, and no hauling required. A grapple separates dirt and rock from the brush first.

**Fence Line Clearing**
Clears brush and cedar along existing or planned fence lines so you can build, inspect, or repair fence. Works with forestry mulching or excavator clearing.

**Site Preparation**
Lot clearing and prep before you build. We clear trees, remove rock, and rough-grade so your builder can start on a clean site.

**Residential Lot Clearing**
Clearing for homesites and residential lots, from house pads and driveways to selective clearing that saves your best trees.

**Commercial Lot Clearing**
Lot clearing for commercial and development sites. Brush, trees, and debris cleared and the site prepped for the next phase.

**Right-of-Way Clearing**
Clears overgrown easements, access roads, and utility right-of-ways with mulching and excavator equipment.

**Dirt Work & Grading**
Dirt work, leveling, and grading for building pads, drainage, and driveways using our own CAT equipment.

**Gravel Driveways & Roads**
Builds and repairs gravel driveways and ranch roads, including clearing, shaping, and base.

**Retaining Walls**
Retaining walls that handle elevation changes, hold back slopes, and stop soil erosion on Hill Country lots.

**Utility Trenching**
Trenching for water, electric, gas, and communication lines on rural and residential properties.

**Demolition**
Demolition and haul-off of old structures, pools, sheds, and slabs. $1,500–$10,000+ depending on size and materials.

**Site Cleanup**
Cleanup of debris, trash, dead trees, and junk left on a property, with haul-off included. Leaves the land ready to use or sell.

**Forestry Mulching Cleanup**
Follow-up cleanup and re-mulching on previously cleared land to knock back regrowth and keep it open.

**Storm Damage Cleanup**
Clears downed trees, limbs, and debris after storms so you can get access back.

**Tree & Stump Removal**
Complete removal of unwanted trees and stumps, root systems included, for a clean, build-ready property.

**ATV Trail Creation**
Cuts ATV and walking trails through brush and cedar with forestry mulching, for hunting access and recreation.
