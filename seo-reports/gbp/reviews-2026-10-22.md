# Review system: 2026-10-22

Current: **18 reviews, 5.0 stars** (Oct 2026). Goal: **2–4 new reviews per month**, every
review answered within 48 hours.

## Rules (breaking these can get reviews removed or the profile suspended)
- **Ask every customer**, not just the happy ones. No "were you satisfied? → then review us" filtering (review gating).
- **No incentives**: no discounts, gift cards, or entries for reviews.
- **No reviews from family, employees, or yourself.** Google treats these as conflicts of interest.
  The "Kelley Arnold" review is from a family member. It's already off the website. If it's also on
  Google, consider asking them to delete it (only the reviewer can), because a review Google flags
  costs more than having one fewer review.
- Don't write reviews for customers or post them from your device or Wi-Fi.

## 1. Get your review link (one time)
1. Search **Grounded Land Services** on Google while logged into the owner account (or open business.google.com).
2. Click **Ask for reviews** (sometimes **Get more reviews**).
3. Copy the short link (looks like `https://g.page/r/…/review`). Paste it into the templates below
   wherever you see `[REVIEW LINK]`.
4. Save it as a phone text shortcut. On iPhone: Settings → Keyboard → Text Replacement →
   shortcut `rvw`. On Android: Gboard → Dictionary → Personal dictionary.
5. Optional: print the QR code from that same screen and keep it on a card in the truck.
6. Send the link to Claude so it can be added to `business-facts.md` and the thank-you page.

## 2. Ask: the day the job wraps
Send it while the customer is still standing on the cleared land. That's when they're happiest.

**Text (send same day):**
```
Hi [First name], this is Logan with Grounded Land Services. Thanks for having us out on your [land clearing / mulching / rock removal] job in [City]! If you have a minute, a Google review helps a small local outfit like ours a ton: [REVIEW LINK]
If anything's not right, text me back here and I'll make it right.
```

**Email (same day, for customers who prefer email):**
```
Subject: Thanks from Grounded Land Services

Hi [First name],

Thanks for trusting us with your property in [City]. It was a good one to work on.

If you have a minute, would you leave us a Google review? It helps other landowners
find a local crew they can trust:
[REVIEW LINK]

If it's helpful, mention what we did (for example, "forestry mulching in Burnet") and
how it turned out. That's totally optional, so write whatever you like.

If anything isn't right, reply to this email or call/text me at (512) 571-6700 and
I'll take care of it.

Thanks again,
Logan
Grounded Land Services
(512) 571-6700
```

## 3. Follow-up: 3 days later (only if no review yet; send once, then stop)
```
Hi [First name], Logan with Grounded Land Services again. Hope the land is looking good! If you get a sec, here's that review link: [REVIEW LINK]. No worries if not, and thanks again for the work.
```

**Tracking:** in `seo-data/leads.csv`, put `review asked` / `review received` in the
`notes` column for each finished job, so the monthly `/seo-track` report shows your ask-to-review rate.

## 4. Reply templates
Personalize every reply: use their first name, the service, the city, and one specific detail
from the job. Don't copy the same reply twice. Never mention prices, addresses, or anything the
customer didn't post themselves.

**5-star**
```
Thanks, [First name]! [Specific detail: "Those big red oaks look great with the cedar cleared out from under them."] We enjoyed working on your [service] project in [City]. Call or text anytime if you need us back.
– Logan, Grounded Land Services
```
Variants: mention the terrain ("that rocky slope didn't make it easy"), the weather ("thanks for
your patience with the rain delay"), or what's next ("good luck with the house build").

**Mixed (3–4 stars)**
```
Thanks for the honest feedback, [First name]. Glad the [service] came out the way you wanted in [City]. I'm sorry [the issue they named: e.g. "the schedule slipped"]. That's on us, and we've [what you changed]. I'd like to hear more. Please call or text me at (512) 571-6700.
– Logan
```

**Negative (1–2 stars)**: wait a few hours, never argue in public, and keep it short.
```
[First name], I'm sorry the job didn't meet your expectations. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to. I'd like to understand what happened and make it right. Please call or text me directly at (512) 571-6700.
– Logan, Grounded Land Services
```
- If you don't recognize the reviewer as a customer: "We can't find a record of working with you.
  Please call (512) 571-6700 so we can look into this." Then use **Report review** in GBP if it
  breaks Google's policies (spam, off-topic, conflict of interest).
- Never reveal job details, prices, or the customer's address in a reply.
