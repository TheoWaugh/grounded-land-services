## Week 2 (Nov 2): Seasonal: why winter is cedar season
**Photo:** `public/images/official/cedar-removal.jpeg` or `work/henson-before-after-mulching.jpeg`
**Button:** Get quote

> Winter is the best time to clear cedar in the Hill Country.
>
> Grass is dormant, so the ground is easy to see and there's less to disturb. Cooler days mean we can run the mulcher longer. And if you have live oaks, getting cedar work done before February matters: the Texas A&M Forest Service recommends avoiding wounding oaks from February through June, when oak wilt spreads most easily.
>
> Cedar (Ashe juniper) soaks up water your oaks and grass need, crowds out pasture, and adds fuel for wildfire. Clearing it opens up views, access, and grazing.
>
> Our options:
> • Forestry mulching: cedar is ground to mulch where it stands, with no burn piles. $2,500–$3,000 per day.
> • Excavator clearing: cedar is grubbed out roots and all. $1,500–$2,500 per acre.
>
> Winter books up fast. Get on the schedule now with a free site visit: call or text (512) 571-6700.

>
> Land clearing: $1,500–$2,500 per acre.

## Week 6 (Nov 30): Seasonal: wildfire prep with fire breaks
**Photo:** `public/images/service-pics/fire-breaks-pic.jpeg` or `fire-breaks-2.jpeg`
**Button:** Learn more → `/services/fire-breaks`

> Dry winter grass plus thick cedar is how Hill Country wildfires spread.
>
> A fire break is a cleared strip with the fuel removed, cut around your home, barn, or property line. It slows a grass fire and gives firefighters a place to work from. We cut fire breaks with our forestry mulcher, which grinds cedar and brush down to mulch at ground level in one pass, with nothing to burn and nothing to haul.
>
> Good places to start:
> • 30+ feet around your house and outbuildings
> • Along fence lines that border thick brush
> • Along driveways and ranch roads so you have a way out
>
> Underbrushing beneath your oaks helps too. It removes the ladder fuel that lets ground fire climb into trees.
>
> Forestry mulching runs $2,500–$3,000 per day. Free site visits: call or text (512) 571-6700.
