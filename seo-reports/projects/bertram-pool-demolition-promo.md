> Pool demolition in Bertram, TX: from pool to backyard in about a week.
>
> This 1,575-square-foot gunite pool had concrete cancer: the steel inside was rusting, and the concrete was cracking and crumbling. The owners wanted a yard again, plus a spot for a jacuzzi.
>
> We took down part of the fence for access, worked carefully in the tight space between the house and the pool, and broke out the pool and patio with our CAT 275 and a rented Bobcat E80 with a hydraulic hammer. Part of the concrete went to build up the owners' shooting berm, which saved on haul-off. Then we backfilled, graded, poured a 10x8 pad for the jacuzzi, and laid 5 pallets of sod.
>
> Demolition runs $1,500–$10,000+ per job depending on size and haul-off, and site visits are free. Call or text (512) 571-6700.
