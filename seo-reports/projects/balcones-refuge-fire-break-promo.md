> A 5-mile fire break off FM 1431 on the Balcones Canyonlands National Wildlife Refuge, close to Leander, Jonestown, Liberty Hill, and our Bertram base.
>
> We built it for the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service in February 2026. Limited access and minimal ground disturbance called for a skid steer mulcher, so we ran our CAT 275 with the Prinoth mulching head and mulched a 5-mile slash pile of cedar and brush in place, on steep inclines, tight access, and rock.
>
> We started Feb 2 and finished before golden-cheeked warbler nesting season began March 1. The fire break also gives firefighters an access route to stop a wildfire before it spreads to more populated areas.
>
> Need a fire break on your property? Site visits are free. Call or text (512) 571-6700.
