# SEO + AIO audit: 2026-10-21

Site: https://www.groundedlandservices.com · Code at commit `7135347` (App Router, Next 16.2.3)

**Method.** I built the site for production and crawled it locally: 408 URLs, made up of 347 sitemap URLs plus every internal link found. For each URL I recorded the status, `<title>`, meta description, canonical, H1/H2s, word count in `<main>`, JSON-LD types (parsed), images missing alt text, and internal links. I checked the live site on 10 URLs. Competitor research used web search plus page fetches. Angi and HomeGuide refused the fetch (403), so they aren't summarized. Web search results aren't a real Google SERP, so the "ranks top 3" findings are approximate. Use a logged-out, location-set Google check to confirm them.

**The live site matches the repo.** The homepage, `/services/land-clearing`, the Liberty Hill city pages, robots.txt, the sitemap and llms.txt all served the same content the build produces. `/service-areas/liberty-hill` returns **404** live as well. The apex domain `groundedlandservices.com` served the homepage. The fetch tool couldn't tell whether that was a 301/308 to www or a duplicate host, so check it with `curl -I https://groundedlandservices.com`.

## Competitor gap

What I found (approximate, see Method):
- **Austin "land clearing"** results are a mix of directories (Angi cost guide, HomeAdvisor), franchises (Monster Tree Service of Greater Austin), local operators (Property Works of Central Texas, HB Land Services), and press-release syndication (PRV Tree Service, Aug 2026). The Angi Austin cost page ranks on a **per-acre cost range + City of Austin tree-permit note**. That's the exact answer-first content our pages lack.
- **Spicewood:** HomeGuide/HomeAdvisor listings (Lone Star Ranchscapes, CS Underbush, Spicewood Land Clearing) dominate. These directory profiles rank on **reviews + "free on-site estimate" + years in business**. Being listed and reviewed there is a citation/backlink task (`/backlink-builder`).
- **Georgetown forestry mulching:** **our GBP listing already shows** in results. The main local rival is **Crosscut Land Clearing**, *also based in Bertram*. Crosscut has a homepage titled "Land Clearing Services in Bertram, Texas" and **per-county service pages** (Travis, Williamson, Burnet, Llano, Hays, Bell…). Its homepage shows ~11 named testimonials with towns, a before/after, owner-led site-visit messaging, machine production claims ("3–5 acres/day dense, up to 10 light"), and mentions **USDA/NRCS grant projects**. It also runs paid press releases per city (e.g. Georgetown, Jan 2026).
- **Liberty Hill:** the search returned only real-estate listings, so the term is under-contested. A strong, unique Liberty Hill hub + case study should win it quickly.

What they have that we don't:
1. **County-level and city-hub pages** (we have city×service only, and the city hub 404s).
2. **Production-rate claims** (acres/day) and **cost-guide content** with per-acre ranges and a permit section.
3. **Local proof on the page**: named testimonials tied to towns, before/after per location.
4. **Program/financing angle** (NRCS EQIP / brush-control cost-share), which we don't mention at all.
5. **Directory presence** (HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, Angi) where Spicewood/Austin queries resolve.

We're ahead on: pricing ranges shown on the page, equipment page with a modern fleet, FAQ schema on service pages, video schema, 18 Google reviews, and case studies (once they're expanded).
